What we do

Choose a service, or call us and we’ll recommend the right one for your car.

Interior detail

Deep vacuum, seat and carpet shampoo, dashboard and console cleaning, vents and door jambs, and streak-free glass.

Exterior wash and polish

Hand wash, wheel and tire cleaning, clay bar treatment, and a polish and wax for lasting shine and protection.

Full inside-and-out detail

Our complete package. Your car is cleaned, restored and sealed from the roof to the floor mats.

Paint correction and ceramic coating

Remove swirls and light scratches, then lock in the gloss with a ceramic coating that protects your paint through Ontario winters.

Headlight restoration

Bring yellowed, foggy headlights back to clear for a cleaner look and better night visibility.

Full body wraps, custom decals and smoke lights

Change your car’s look with a full body wrap, custom decals, or smoked lights.
